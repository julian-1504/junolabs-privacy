# Privacy policy for Summit Rush

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · [Deutsche Fassung](datenschutz.html)

## Who is responsible

Julian Alt

E-mail: junolabs@julian-alt.de

Write to this address with any question about privacy.

## In short

Summit Rush collects no data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app
purchases. The game does not use the internet and asks for no Android permissions. The
publisher receives nothing from you.

## What is stored on your device

Your progress and your settings are kept in one file in the app's private storage on your
device:

- **Progress:** coins, rank, vehicles and their upgrades, paints, drivers, gadgets, unlocked
  stages, records, missions, achievements and statistics
- **Settings:** volume, vibration, graphics quality, language, pedal size and left-handed mode

Other apps on the device cannot read this file.

## Android backup

Backup is a feature of Android itself: if backup is turned on for your device, Android saves
the game's save file to **your** Google account and restores it on a new device. The publisher
has no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android's settings; Google's handling is
covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How long data is kept

Until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings. "Reset progress" in the
game's settings resets your progress and keeps your settings.

## What the game does not do

- No advertising and no advertising SDKs
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics
- No advertising ID and no device fingerprinting
- No internet connection and no sharing of data with third parties

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data
portability and objection. The publisher holds no data about you; you can delete your save
yourself at any time, as described above. If you still have a question, write to
junolabs@julian-alt.de. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory
authority.

## Age

Summit Rush is meant for players aged 13 and older. The game does not ask for your age, a name
or an e-mail address.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a new version of the game changes
anything stated here, this policy is updated before that version is released.
